## Thesis
Asana is transforming enterprise productivity by integrating AI "teammates" that function as true, multiplayer actors within the organization’s existing work ecosystem. By leveraging Anthropic’s Cloud managed agents, Asana enables these AI agents to access deep enterprise context—such as historical decisions, project goals, and workflows—allowing them to execute complex, multistep tasks while maintaining security, auditability, and human-in-the-loop collaboration.

## Key Points
*   **Vision for Agentic Enterprise:** Asana’s goal is to move beyond "single-player" AI experimentation toward a "multiplayer" model where AI agents act as collaborative teammates that work alongside humans to complete end-to-end workflows.
*   **Leveraging Enterprise Context:** Asana utilizes its 17-year-old "work graph"—which maps mission, goals, portfolios, and tasks—to provide AI agents with the necessary context to make informed, accurate decisions that align with specific company strategies.
*   **Integration of Cloud Managed Agents:** By using Anthropic’s managed agents, Asana has significantly reduced prototyping costs and improved output quality through built-in verification loops and graders that iterate on tasks until they meet high standards.
*   **Multiplayer Functionality:** The system allows multiple human users to interact with the same agent, providing feedback and "nudges" that are saved into the agent's memory, ensuring that knowledge compounds and improves over time for the entire team.
*   **Security and Governance:** Asana maintains strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and auditability, ensuring that agents act as secure, managed teammates that do not leak information across different projects or work graph objects.
*   **Pre-built AI Teammates:** As of March, Asana has released over 21 pre-built AI teammates tailored for specific roles like PMO, marketing, IT, and HR, capable of performing tasks like launch planning, spec writing, and resource management.
*   **Real-World Application:** In a demonstration, a marketer used an AI teammate to generate a campaign brief and an HTML landing page mock-up, which was then iteratively refined through simple, natural language comments from other team members.
*   **Future Roadmap:** Asana is focusing on increasing agent proactivity, where AI teammates will autonomously identify issues or suggest remediation steps based on project patterns, even without being explicitly assigned a task.
*   **Third-Party Integrations:** Asana manages third-party tool integrations (such as Google Drive or Office 365) at both the agent loop level and the MCP (Model Context Protocol) level to ensure agents have comprehensive access to external data.
